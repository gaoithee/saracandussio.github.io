## About Me

**Hello there**, I'm Sara!

I'm a second-year PhD student in Natural Language Processing at the University of Trieste (Italy), where I work within the [AI Lab](https://ai-lab.units.it/) under the supervision of [Luca Bortolussi](https://ai-lab.units.it/?page_id=139) and [Gabriele Sarti](https://gsarti.com/). I was previously a visiting researcher at the [Center for Language and Cognition (CLCG)](https://www.rug.nl/research/clcg/) at the University of Groningen. 🌍

My research focuses on **reasoning in large language models**, understanding how LLMs generalize, identifying their limitations, and exploring ways to push them beyond memorization. I'm particularly interested in Reinforcement Learning paradigms and Neuro-Symbolic integration. 🧠

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Statistics and a Master's degree in Data Science and AI (both 110/110 cum laude). My Master's thesis explored how multi-agent systems can enhance the performance of compact models on Question Answering tasks, and was awarded a **Mention of Honor** at CLiC-it 2025.

Outside of research, I'm vice-president of the [AI Student Society (AI2S)](https://ai2s.it/) and department representative for the Mathematics, Informatics, and Geosciences section at the University of Trieste.
